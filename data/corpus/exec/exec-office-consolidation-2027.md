# 2027 Office Consolidation and Restructuring Plan

**Restricted. Leadership team and board only.**
**Status:** Approved in principle by the board on 22 October 2026. Final approval at the December 2026 board meeting.

## 1. Decision

1. **Close the Toronto office** on **30 June 2027**.
2. **Reduce headcount by about 8% (approximately 34 roles)** across the company.
3. Keep Austin (HQ) and London. Move London from a serviced office to a smaller one when the notice period allows.
4. Announce on **Wednesday 24 February 2027**.

## 2. Why

- Toronto has 58 employees, mainly in Engineering (Ledger) and Customer Success. Most Toronto work duplicates functions in Austin.
- The Toronto lease (to 2029) has a **break clause in 2027**: break fee $1.1M.
- The 2027 plan targets **cash-flow breakeven in Q4 2027** and needs the savings, especially with Project Falcon using $30M of cash.
- Engagement in Toronto is the lowest of all offices (engagement survey).

## 3. Financials

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annualised savings | **$6.8M** (Toronto facilities $0.74M; roles $6.06M) |
| One-off costs | **$3.9M** (severance $2.8M, lease break $1.1M) |
| Payback | about 7 months |

## 4. People impact

| Group | Roles affected |
|---|---|
| Toronto: eliminated | ~22 |
| Toronto: offered remote-Canada or relocation to Austin | ~36 |
| Austin, London, remote-US | ~12 |
| **Total roles eliminated** | **~34** |

- Selection criteria and severance: see the Head of People's *RIF Selection Criteria* paper.
- Severance: 4 weeks + 2 weeks per year of service (US formula), statutory or better elsewhere; executive terms are separate.
- No UK collective consultation required (fewer than 20 UK roles).

## 5. Things we must stop doing now (without explaining why)

- No new Toronto hires; Toronto requisitions on hold (VP Engineering informed).
- No relocations into Toronto (People team informed).
- Toronto promotions held until checked against the list.
- No Toronto-based recipients of 2027 equity refresh grants until the list is final.

## 6. Communication plan

| Date | Audience | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 2026 | Board | Final approval |
| 23 Feb 2027 | Managers of affected employees | Briefing under confidentiality |
| **24 Feb 2027, morning** | Affected employees | Individual meetings |
| 24 Feb 2027, 14:00 CT | All employees | CEO all-hands; written FAQ |
| 24 Feb 2027 | Customers with Toronto-based CSMs | Account managers call each customer |

Until 24 February, the approved answer to questions about offices or layoffs is the one used at the Q3 all-hands: "Our office footprint is reviewed every year as part of planning, and any changes would be communicated well in advance."

## 7. Risks

- **Leaks before announcement.** Keep the list of people who know to the leadership team, People leadership and Legal.
- Customer impact from CSM changes. Mitigation: warm handover plans for each account.
- Retention of critical Ledger engineers in Toronto. Mitigation: relocation and remote offers made on the day.
