# Minutes of the Meeting of the Board of Directors of Acme Inc.

**Date:** 22 October 2026, 09:00–13:30 CT
**Location:** Austin office and video conference
**Present (directors):** Henry Park (CEO), three investor directors, two independent directors (including the Audit Committee chair)
**Attending:** Frank Osei (CFO), Grace Liu (General Counsel, secretary), Priya Nair (VP Engineering, item 4 only)

## 1. Minutes of the previous meeting
The minutes of the meeting of 23 July 2026 were approved.

## 2. CEO and financial report
The CEO presented the Q3 board pack. Revenue was $18.4M (+31% YoY), ARR $74M, net burn $3.1M. The board noted results above plan. One director asked about Ledger's path to $12M ARR; the CEO said multi-currency and the Corvid ERP connector are the main drivers.

## 3. Project Falcon
The CFO presented the Lumen Analytics opportunity. Directors discussed valuation (12× ARR), cash headroom against the Crestline covenant, and integration risk.
**Resolved:** to authorise management to sign a non-binding LOI (subsequently signed on 6 November 2026) and approve legal and diligence costs up to $600,000. Final approval to come to the board in February 2027.
*One investor director abstained, citing cash headroom.*

## 4. INC-2041
The VP Engineering summarised the root cause and the completed action items. The General Counsel gave a privileged update on customer impact (37 customers), the late notice to Halcyon Bank, and estimated exposure of $1.2M–$2.5M.
**Resolved:** to authorise settlement with Halcyon Bank up to $1,000,000.
The Audit Committee chair asked for security incident timing to be added to the quarterly Audit Committee report.

## 5. 2027 office consolidation and restructuring
The CEO and CFO presented the plan to close the Toronto office by 30 June 2027 and reduce headcount by about 34 roles, with annualised savings of $6.8M.
**Resolved:** to approve the plan in principle, with final approval at the December meeting. Announcement planned for 24 February 2027.
Directors asked management to make sure the selection process is reviewed by Legal for adverse impact and that whistleblower matters are handled separately.

## 6. Executive compensation (Compensation Committee report)
The Compensation Committee chair reported the approval of the updated Executive Leave & Compensation Policy (unlimited PTO and an 8-week sabbatical for executives, 12-month severance) and of the 2027 executive base salaries.

## 7. CEO succession (executive session, management not present except the CEO for part)
The board reviewed the CEO succession plan. Details recorded in the confidential succession paper.

## 8. Any other business
None. The meeting closed at 13:30.

*Signed: Grace Liu, Secretary*
