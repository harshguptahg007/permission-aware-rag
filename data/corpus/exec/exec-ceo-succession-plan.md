# CEO and Leadership Succession Plan

**Highly restricted. Board, CEO and Head of People only.** Reviewed annually by the board in executive session (last review: 22 October 2026).

## 1. Emergency succession (unexpected absence of the CEO)

| Period | Interim leader |
|---|---|
| First 30 days | **Frank Osei (CFO)** as acting CEO |
| Beyond 30 days | Board decides: extend interim or start search |

The acting CEO has full authority except for M&A, financing and restructuring decisions, which need board approval.

## 2. Long-term CEO succession

| Candidate | Readiness | Development needs |
|---|---|---|
| Priya Nair (VP Engineering) | Ready in **2–3 years** | Commercial and board exposure; P&L ownership |
| Frank Osei (CFO) | Ready now as interim; **not** a preferred long-term candidate | Product vision |
| External | — | The board will run an external search alongside internal candidates when the time comes |

Henry Park has told the board he intends to remain CEO **through at least 2028**.

## 3. Other leadership roles

| Role | Emergency backup | Ready-now successor | Ready in 1–2 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| CFO | Controller | None | FP&A lead |
| General Counsel | Kessler Ward LLP (outside counsel) | None | Senior commercial counsel |
| VP Engineering | Director of Engineering (Beacon) | Mei Tanaka (Staff), for a narrower scope | Director of Engineering (Beacon) |
| Head of People | Senior People Partner | None | Senior People Partner |
| VP Sales | Director, Enterprise Sales (London) | None | — |

## 4. Planned changes

- **Chief Operating Officer** to be hired in H2 2027. Part of the development plan for Priya Nair includes taking on more cross-functional scope after the COO is hired.
- VP Sales role becomes SVP Sales reporting to the COO.

## 5. Retention

- Mei Tanaka: 25,000-option retention grant in the 2027 equity refresh programme.
- Priya Nair: executive retention grant approved by the board in 2026.
- Double-trigger acceleration applies to all executives in a change of control.
