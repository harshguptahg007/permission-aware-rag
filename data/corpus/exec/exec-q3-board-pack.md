# Q3 2026 Board Pack

**Board meeting:** 22 October 2026
**Prepared by:** Henry Park (CEO) and Frank Osei (CFO)
**Distribution:** Board of directors and leadership team only.

## 1. CEO summary

Q3 was our strongest quarter for revenue and the hardest quarter for trust. Revenue grew **31% year over year to $18.4M**, ARR reached **$74M**, and Beacon passed 1,000 customers. Net burn fell to **$3.1M**. Against that, the INC-2041 storage exposure affected 37 customers and puts one large renewal at risk.

Three decisions for this meeting:
1. Approve signing a non-binding **LOI for Project Falcon** (Lumen Analytics) and starting diligence.
2. Approve in principle the **2027 office consolidation and restructuring** (detail in a separate paper).
3. Note the INC-2041 legal update and remediation status.
