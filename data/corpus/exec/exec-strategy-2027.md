# Acme Strategy 2027

**Leadership team only. Draft for the December board meeting.**

## Where we want to be at the end of 2027

- **$101M ARR** (from $79M forecast at the end of 2026).
- **Cash-flow breakeven in Q4 2027.**
- Beacon recognised as the observability platform for **regulated industries** (financial services, healthcare).
- Ledger at **$12M ARR** with multi-currency and two ERP connectors.

## Four bets

### 1. Regulated-industry Beacon
- EU data residency (Frankfurt) in Q3 2027.
- Customer-managed encryption keys.
- SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA-ready, and a published security programme to rebuild trust after INC-2041.

### 2. AI in Beacon (accelerated by Project Falcon)
- Acquire Lumen Analytics (Falcon) for anomaly detection and ML talent; closing Q2 2027.
- Ship AI log summaries in Q2 2027 and Lumen-based anomaly detection by Q4 2027.

### 3. Pricing and packaging
- Move Beacon from per-host to **per-GB ingest pricing** on 1 April 2027 (see the pricing change paper).
- Expected effect: +6% ARR from existing customers over 12 months.

### 4. Efficiency
- Office consolidation (close Toronto, ~34 roles): $6.8M annualised savings.
- Infrastructure cost per GB down 25%.
- Hiring limited to 6 new engineering roles plus Falcon.

## What we will not do in 2027

- No new offices.
- No on-premise product.
- No new product lines outside Beacon and Ledger, other than Falcon capabilities.

## Company OKRs (draft)

| Objective | Key results |
|---|---|
| Grow in regulated industries | 15 new financial-services and healthcare logos; NRR ≥ 118% |
| Earn back trust | Zero SEV1 security incidents; SOC 2 Type II with no exceptions |
| Make AI real in Beacon | 30% of Enterprise customers using AI summaries by Q4 |
| Reach breakeven | Q4 2027 cash-flow breakeven; burn ≤ $5.5M for the year |

## Leadership changes

- Hire a **Chief Operating Officer** in H2 2027 to run Sales, Customer Success and Operations (see the CEO succession paper).
- Marcus Bell (VP Sales) to be told in January; role to be offered as SVP Sales reporting to the COO.
