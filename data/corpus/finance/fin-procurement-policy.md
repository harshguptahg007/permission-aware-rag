# Procurement Policy

**Owner:** Procurement (Finance)

## Purpose

Make sure Acme buys the right things at a fair price, from vendors that are safe to work with, and with the right approvals.

## Approval thresholds (total contract value)

| Value | Quotes required | Approvals |
|---|---|---|
| Under $10,000 | None | Budget owner |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | **2 quotes** | Budget owner + department director |
| $50,000 – $250,000 | **3 quotes** or a sole-source justification | Budget owner + CFO + Legal contract review |
| Over $250,000 | Formal RFP | CFO + CEO + Legal |

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid a threshold is a breach of this policy.

## Security and privacy review

Any vendor that will **store or process customer data or employee personal data** needs:
- a security review by the Security team (questionnaire + SOC 2 report or equivalent);
- a **data processing agreement** reviewed by Legal.

No customer data can be shared with a vendor before both are complete.

## Conflicts of interest

Anyone involved in choosing a vendor must declare any personal or family relationship with that vendor (see POL-GEN-006). Procurement re-tenders a contract if an undisclosed conflict is found.

## Contracts

- Only the CFO, CEO and General Counsel can sign contracts over $50,000.
- Auto-renewal clauses need a calendar reminder 90 days before renewal, owned by the budget owner.
- Standard payment terms are net 30.

## Preferred vendors

Use preferred vendors where they exist (laptops, travel through TripDesk, recruiting agencies, legal counsel). The list is on the Finance intranet page.

## Software purchases

Software is bought through the IT portal, not by individuals (see POL-FIN-002, section 7). IT checks for overlapping tools before purchase.
