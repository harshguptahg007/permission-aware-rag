# Annual Budget and Reforecast Process

**Owner:** FP&A (Finance)
**Audience:** Finance team and budget owners

## Budget owners

Every cost centre has one **budget owner**, usually the department head. Budget owners are accountable for spend against plan. Finance partners with each owner but does not own their budget.

## Annual budget calendar

| When | Step |
|---|---|
| Early September | Leadership sets top-down targets (revenue, burn, headcount) |
| Mid September | FP&A sends templates in **PlanRight** (the planning tool) |
| End of September | Budget owners submit headcount and non-headcount plans |
| October | Review meetings: each owner with CFO and FP&A |
| November | Leadership consolidates and trades off; operating plan drafted |
| December | Board approves the annual operating plan |
| January | Budgets loaded into PlanRight and the ERP; monthly tracking begins |

## Headcount planning

- Headcount is planned by role, level, location and start month.
- Fully loaded cost = base salary + **25%** (benefits, payroll taxes, equipment, software).
- New hires are assumed to start mid-month in their planned month.
- Backfills are **not automatic**; they need to be in plan or approved by the CFO.

## Quarterly reforecast

- After each quarter closes, FP&A updates the forecast for the rest of the year.
- Budget owners explain any variance above **10%** or **$50,000** (whichever is larger) against plan.
- The reforecast is presented to leadership in the second week after quarter close.

## Rules for budget owners

- You can move money **within** your cost centre without approval.
- Moving money **between** cost centres needs CFO approval.
- Unspent budget doesn't carry over to the next year.
- Any new recurring commitment above **$25,000 per year** needs a Finance partner review before signature (see the Procurement Policy).

## Reports

Budget owners receive a monthly **budget vs actual** report by the 8th business day of the following month.
