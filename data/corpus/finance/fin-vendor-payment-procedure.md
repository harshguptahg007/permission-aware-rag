# Vendor Payment Procedure

**Owner:** Accounts Payable (Finance)

## Payment terms

- Standard terms: **net 30** from invoice date.
- Early-payment discounts are taken when the discount is at least 1% for paying 20 days early.
- Shorter terms need Controller approval.

## How invoices are processed

1. Vendors send invoices to the AP inbox. Invoices sent to individuals are forwarded to AP.
2. AP matches each invoice to an approved **purchase order** (PO) and confirms receipt of goods or services with the requester (**3-way match**).
3. Invoices without a PO are returned to the vendor unless they are on the *no-PO list* (utilities, payroll providers, government fees).
4. Approved invoices are scheduled for the next payment run.

## Payment runs

- **ACH / bank transfer:** every **Thursday**.
- **International wires:** every second Thursday, or on request for urgent payments with Controller approval.
- Payment runs are prepared by an AP specialist and **released by a second person** (Controller or CFO). Nobody can create and release the same payment.

## Changes to vendor bank details

Bank detail changes are the most common route for payment fraud. Follow this procedure **every time**, with no exceptions:

1. A change request must come through the vendor portal or on vendor letterhead.
2. AP **calls the vendor back** on the phone number already on file (not a number in the request) to confirm the change.
3. A second AP team member verifies the call note and updates the vendor record.
4. The first payment to new bank details is limited to **$5,000** or the invoice amount, whichever is lower, and the vendor confirms receipt before further payments.

Never change bank details based on an email alone, even if it appears to come from a senior Acme leader. Executives will never ask AP to bypass this procedure.

## Urgent payments

Urgent same-day payments need:
- a written request from a budget owner;
- Controller approval;
- and, above **$50,000**, CFO approval in the payments system (not by email or chat).

## Records

All invoices, POs and approvals are kept in the ERP for **7 years**.
