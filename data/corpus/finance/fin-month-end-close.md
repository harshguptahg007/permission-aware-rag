# Month-End Close Checklist

**Owner:** Controller
**Target:** books closed by **business day 5 (BD5)**.

## BD-2 to BD0 (before month end)

- [ ] Remind budget owners to submit outstanding POs and receipts.
- [ ] Remind employees to submit Spendly expenses for the month.
- [ ] Confirm payroll for the month is final.

## BD1

- [ ] Close AP sub-ledger; accrue for goods and services received but not invoiced.
- [ ] Import Spendly expenses and corporate card transactions.
- [ ] Run Ledger and Beacon billing reports; reconcile invoiced amounts to the ERP.

## BD2

- [ ] Bank reconciliations for all accounts (US, Canada, UK).
- [ ] Revenue: post ratable subscription revenue, usage revenue, deferred revenue roll-forward.
- [ ] Payroll journal entries, including bonus and commission accruals.

## BD3

- [ ] Prepaid expense amortisation (software, insurance).
- [ ] Fixed assets and depreciation (laptops capitalised above **$2,500**).
- [ ] Capitalised commissions amortisation.
- [ ] Intercompany reconciliation (Acme Inc., Acme Canada Ltd., Acme UK Ltd.) and FX revaluation.

## BD4

- [ ] Flux analysis: explain any P&L line that moved more than **10% and $25,000** from the previous month.
- [ ] Controller review of all reconciliations.

## BD5

- [ ] Close the period in the ERP.
- [ ] Publish the management P&L to the CFO.
- [ ] Budget vs actual reports to budget owners by BD8.

## Quarter-end additions

- Revenue cut-off testing on a sample of 20 contracts.
- Stock-based compensation expense update.
- Board reporting pack inputs to FP&A by **BD8**.

## Year-end additions

- External audit preparation (auditors on site in February).
- 1099 forms (US contractors) by 31 January.
