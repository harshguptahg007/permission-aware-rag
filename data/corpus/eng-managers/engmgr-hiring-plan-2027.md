## Notes

- **No new roles in Toronto in 2027.** Toronto requisitions are on hold pending a leadership decision. Don't discuss this with candidates or staff.
- Backfills for leavers need VP approval and a short case; the default is not to backfill.
- The plan prefers Austin and remote-US for cost reasons.
- Contractor spend is being reduced; Northbridge engagement ends after the Corvid connector ships.

## Interview loop changes

- All engineering loops move to the new structured rubric in January.
- Each loop: recruiter screen, technical screen, system design, coding, values interview.
- Hiring managers make the decision with a debrief; no single interviewer can veto.

## Budget

Engineering hiring budget for 2027 is set by Finance; recruiter fees above 15% need Finance approval.
