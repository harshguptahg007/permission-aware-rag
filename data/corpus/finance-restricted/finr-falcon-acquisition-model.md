# Project Falcon: Acquisition Financial Model Summary

**Restricted. CFO, CEO and General Counsel only. Subject to NDA.**
Codename **Falcon** = **Lumen Analytics, Inc.** Do not use the target's name in email subjects, calendar invites or Slack.

## Target

| | |
|---|---|
| Company | Lumen Analytics, Inc. (Seattle) |
| Product | AI-based anomaly detection for time-series and log data |
| Employees | 28 (22 engineers) |
| ARR | $3.5M, growing ~60% YoY |
| Gross margin | 64% |
| Net burn | $0.7M per quarter |

## Rationale

1. Accelerates the Beacon AI roadmap (AI log summaries, anomaly detection) by an estimated 12–18 months.
2. Adds a team of 22 engineers with ML experience, which we cannot hire quickly.
3. Cross-sell to Beacon's 1,000+ customers.

## Deal terms (from the LOI)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Enterprise value | **$42M** |
| Cash at closing | $30M |
| Acme stock | $12M (at the latest preferred price) |
| Retention pool | $4M in Acme RSUs/options for Lumen employees, vesting 3 years |
| Earn-out | None |
| Exclusivity | 60 days from LOI signing (6 Nov 2026) |

## Valuation

- 12× current ARR, compared with 8–15× for recent comparable AI-observability acquisitions.
- DCF base case: **$47M** (WACC 16%, terminal growth 4%).
- Downside case (cross-sell fails): **$31M**.

## Synergies (year 2)

- Revenue: $6M ARR from cross-sell to Beacon customers.
- Cost: $1.5M from moving Lumen to Acme's AWS contracts and removing duplicate tools.

## Financing

- $30M cash from balance sheet (cash at end of Q3: $41M).
- Post-closing cash would fall to about **$11M**. The minimum-cash covenant with Crestline Bank is **$10M**, so the venture debt facility may need to be drawn or the covenant renegotiated.

## Timeline

| Date | Step |
|---|---|

## Key risks

- Lumen's two largest customers are 35% of its ARR.
- Security due diligence must be completed with extra care after INC-2041.
- Cash covenant headroom (see above).
