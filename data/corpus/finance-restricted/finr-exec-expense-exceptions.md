# Executive Expense & Travel Exceptions

**Confidential. CFO and Controller only.**
**Applies to:** VP-level and above. Supplements the Expense Policy (POL-FIN-002) and the Travel Booking Guide (POL-FIN-008).
**Approved by:** CEO and the board's Compensation Committee, September 2026.

## Exceptions to POL-FIN-002

| Item | Standard policy | Executive exception |
|---|---|---|
| Flights | Economy < 6 h, premium economy ≥ 6 h | **Business class for flights over 4 hours** |
| Hotels | $250/night ($325 high-cost cities) | **$450/night** in any city |
| Meals | $75/day domestic, $100 international | No daily cap; "reasonable" standard applies |
| Client meals | $120 per person | $250 per person |
| Ground transport | Public transport, rideshare, taxi | **Car service** allowed |
| Approval | Manager ≤ $2,000, VP above | CFO approves all executive expenses; CEO expenses approved by the Audit Committee chair |

## Additional allowances

- **Partner travel:** an executive's partner may travel to board dinners and the annual leadership off-site at Acme's expense (taxable benefit).
- **Airport lounges:** annual lounge membership reimbursed.
- **Home security** for the CEO: up to $15,000 per year.

## Controls

- Executive expenses are still submitted in Spendly with receipts above $25.
- The Controller reviews all executive expenses quarterly and reports totals to the Audit Committee.
- Total executive travel and expenses for 2026 (to end of Q3): **$412,000**, of which CEO **$138,000**.

## Disclosure

These exceptions are not published in the company handbook. If asked, the approved response is: "Executive expenses are approved by the CFO and reviewed by the Audit Committee."
