# Venture Debt Facility and Banking Covenants

**Confidential. CFO and Controller only.**

## Facility

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lender | Crestline Bank |
| Facility size | **$15M** term loan, undrawn |
| Draw period | Until 30 June 2027 |
| Interest | Prime + 1.5%, interest-only for 12 months after drawing, then 30-month amortisation |
| Warrants | 0.25% of fully diluted shares issued to the lender at signing |
| Security | First lien on all assets except IP (negative pledge on IP) |

## Financial covenants (tested monthly)

1. **Minimum cash:** Acme must hold at least **$10M** in unrestricted cash at Crestline Bank at all times.
2. **Revenue performance:** trailing-three-month revenue must be at least **80% of the board-approved plan**.
3. **Reporting:** monthly financial statements within 30 days of month end; annual audited statements within 150 days of year end.

## Other restrictions

- No acquisitions above **$10M** without the lender's consent.
- No additional debt above $2M.
- No dividends.
- A material adverse change (for example, a major security incident with significant customer loss) may let the lender refuse to fund a draw.
