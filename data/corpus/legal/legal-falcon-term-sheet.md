# Project Falcon: Letter of Intent Summary and Legal Workplan

**Privileged & confidential. Subject to NDA with the target.** General Counsel, CEO and CFO only.

## Parties

- **Buyer:** Acme Inc.
- **Target:** Lumen Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation headquartered in Seattle ("Falcon").
- **Target counsel:** Pike & Lowell LLP
- **Acme counsel:** Kessler Ward LLP (M&A team)

## LOI key terms (signed 6 November 2026)

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Structure | Reverse triangular merger; Lumen becomes a wholly owned subsidiary |
| Price | **$42M** enterprise value: $30M cash + $12M Acme stock |
| Working capital | Normalised working capital target; dollar-for-dollar adjustment |
| Escrow | 10% of consideration ($4.2M) held **15 months** for general indemnity claims |
| Special indemnity | Uncapped for fundamental reps; IP reps capped at 50% of consideration |
| Retention | $4M pool for Lumen employees; 5 key employees must sign offer letters before signing |
| Exclusivity | **60 days** (to 5 January 2027), extendable by 30 days by mutual agreement |
| Binding provisions | Exclusivity, confidentiality, expenses only; the rest is non-binding |

## Key employees (must sign before signing)

1. CEO and co-founder
2. CTO and co-founder
3. Head of ML
4. Two senior ML engineers

## Diligence workplan

| Workstream | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate and cap table | Kessler Ward | 4 Dec 2026 |
| Commercial contracts (top 20 customers) | Acme Legal | 11 Dec 2026 |
| IP: ownership, open-source licence scan, invention assignments | Acme Legal + Engineering | 18 Dec 2026 |
| Security and privacy (incl. SOC 2 status) | Acme Security | 18 Dec 2026 |
| Employment (classification, visas, equity) | Acme People + Kessler Ward | 18 Dec 2026 |
| Financial and tax | CFO + external accountants | 18 Dec 2026 |

## Regulatory

- HSR (US antitrust) filing **not required**: deal value is below the 2026 threshold.
- No foreign-investment review expected.

## Consents

- **Crestline Bank** consent is needed because the deal exceeds $10M (venture debt covenant). CFO owns this.
- Lumen has two customer contracts with change-of-control clauses; consents to be obtained before closing.

## Confidentiality rules

- Use only the codename "Falcon" internally.
- Engineering involvement is limited to two named senior engineers under a separate NDA for technical diligence.
- Never mention Falcon in company-wide channels, all-hands, or recruiting conversations.
