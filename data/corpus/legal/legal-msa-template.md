# Master Subscription Agreement (MSA): Standard Terms and Negotiation Playbook

**Owner:** Legal
**Audience:** Legal team. Sales receives the approved fallback positions through deal desk.

## Standard terms

| Clause | Standard position |
|---|---|
| Term | 12 months, auto-renews for 12 months unless 60 days' notice |
| Fees | Annual in advance; net 30 |
| Uptime SLA | **99.9%** monthly (Beacon and Ledger) |
| SLA credits | 5% of monthly fee per 0.1% below SLA, capped at 25% |
| Liability cap | **12 months of fees** paid or payable |
| Excluded from cap | Indemnities, breach of confidentiality, fraud |
| Data breach super-cap | 2× annual fees for breaches of data protection obligations |
| Security incident notice | **72 hours** from confirmation of the incident |
| Data processing | Standard DPA attached; UK/EU SCCs where needed |
| Governing law | Delaware (US customers); England & Wales (UK customers) |
| Data retention after termination | 30 days for export, then deletion |

## Negotiation playbook

| Customer ask | Fallback 1 (deal desk can approve) | Fallback 2 (General Counsel only) | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| Higher liability cap | 18 months of fees | 24 months | Unlimited liability |
| Shorter breach notice | 48 hours from confirmation | 48 hours from discovery | Anything under 48 hours, or "immediately" |
| 99.95% SLA | Allowed for Enterprise with dedicated tier | — | 99.99% |
| Termination for convenience | With 90 days' notice, no refund of prepaid fees | Pro-rata refund | Refund without notice |
| Most-favoured-customer pricing | No | No | Yes |
| Source code escrow | Ledger only, customer pays escrow fees | — | Beacon |

## Lessons from INC-2041

- Some older contracts (for example, with financial-services customers) have custom security addenda with **72 hours from discovery** notice periods. These are stricter than the standard. Legal is reviewing all custom addenda; new deals must use **"from confirmation"** wording.
- Sales must not agree to custom security addenda without Legal review.

## Signature authority

MSAs and order forms up to $250,000 per year can be signed by the CFO; above that, by the CEO. The General Counsel can sign any MSA.
