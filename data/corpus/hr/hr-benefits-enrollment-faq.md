# Benefits Enrolment FAQ

Prepared answers for the People team to use in #ask-people during open enrolment (1–15 November). Copy, adapt, and link to the Benefits Overview (POL-HR-011).

**Q: I missed open enrolment. Can I still change my plan?**
Only with a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption, divorce, loss of other coverage) reported in PeopleHub within **30 days** of the event. Otherwise, changes wait until next November.

**Q: What's the difference between the PPO and HDHP in the US?**
The PPO has higher premiums and lower deductibles. The HDHP has lower premiums, a higher deductible, and Acme puts **$1,000 a year** into the employee's HSA. Point people to the plan comparison sheet in PeopleHub.

**Q: Can I add my partner?**
Yes, spouses and domestic partners can be added. Domestic partner coverage in the US may create taxable imputed income; refer to payroll for details.

**Q: How do I claim the wellbeing allowance?**
$75 per month, claimed in Spendly under *Wellbeing*. Unused months don't roll over.

**Q: Is there a 401(k) match during parental leave?**
Yes. Pay continues during parental leave, so contributions and the 4% match continue.

**Q: Can I change my pension contribution in the UK?**
Yes, any time in PeopleHub. Acme contributes 5% regardless of your contribution (minimum employee contribution applies for auto-enrolment).

**Q: Do contractors get benefits?**
No. Contractors are covered by their agency.

**Q: Who do I escalate claim disputes to?**
First to Northwind Health member services. If unresolved after 30 days, the People team's benefits lead can escalate through the broker.

**Q: When do changes take effect?**
Open-enrolment changes take effect on **1 January**. Life-event changes take effect from the date of the event.
