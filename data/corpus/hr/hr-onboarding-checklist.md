# New Hire Onboarding Checklist (HR Operations)

The People team's internal checklist. The new hire sees the *Welcome to Acme* guide; this is what we do behind the scenes.

## When the offer is signed (T-minus 3 weeks or more)

- [ ] Create the employee record in **PeopleHub** with start date, manager, level, location and pay.
- [ ] Send the right-to-work / I-9 / identity verification request.
- [ ] Order the background check if not already done.
- [ ] Request a laptop and accounts from IT (IT ticket template "New hire").
- [ ] Assign an **onboarding buddy** with the hiring manager.
- [ ] Book the new hire into the next **New Hire Orientation**.

## Week before start (T-minus 5 days)

- [ ] Confirm the laptop has shipped (remote) or is ready at the office.
- [ ] Send the welcome email with day-1 logistics.
- [ ] Remind the manager to plan the first week and 30/60/90-day goals.
- [ ] Add the new hire to payroll for the next run.

## Day 1

- [ ] Verify identity documents in person or by video call.
- [ ] Confirm PeopleHub profile, bank details and tax forms are complete.
- [ ] Assign mandatory training: security awareness, privacy basics, anti-harassment, Code of Conduct acknowledgement.

## First 30 days

- [ ] Benefits enrolment completed (deadline: **30 days** from start).
- [ ] Mandatory training completed (deadline: **14 days**).
- [ ] 30-day check-in survey sent to the new hire.

## Day 60 and day 90

- [ ] Prompt manager for the 60-day and 90-day probation / introductory check-ins.
- [ ] At day 90, manager confirms completion of probation in PeopleHub. If there are concerns, the People partner joins the 90-day conversation.
- [ ] Trigger the referral bonus payment if the hire came from a referral.

## Country-specific

- **US:** I-9 within 3 business days of start; state tax forms.
- **Canada:** SIN collected; TD1 forms.
- **UK:** Right-to-work check before day 1; P45 or starter checklist; pension auto-enrolment.

## Contractors

Contractors are **not** onboarded through this checklist. Their access is requested by the Acme partner manager, and their agency handles employment matters.
