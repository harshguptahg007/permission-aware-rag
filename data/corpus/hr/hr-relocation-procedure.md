# Relocation Procedure

**Owner:** People team
**Applies to:** New hires and existing employees moving at Acme's request or with Acme's approval.

## Packages

| Package | Who | Lump sum | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic | Moves within the same country, over 50 miles | **$5,000** (CAD 6,500 / £4,000) | — |
| International | Moves between countries | **$15,000** (CAD 20,000 / £12,000) | Visa costs, 30 days temporary housing, one home-finding trip |
| Executive | Director and above | Negotiated case by case | Approved by Head of People |

Lump sums are paid with the first payroll after the move and are taxable.

## Repayment

If the employee resigns or is dismissed for cause within **12 months** of the relocation payment, they repay:
- 100% if they leave within 6 months;
- 50% if they leave between 6 and 12 months.

The repayment agreement must be signed **before** any payment.

## Process

1. The hiring manager or employee requests relocation through the People partner.
2. The People partner checks the new location's salary band and anchor-day requirements (POL-HR-004, POL-HR-005).
3. For international moves, start the visa process first (see *Visa Sponsorship Process*).
4. Send the relocation agreement through PeopleHub for signature.
5. HR operations updates the employee's location, pay band location, payroll entity and benefits.

## Employee-requested moves

Employees who move for personal reasons (not at Acme's request) get **no relocation package**. Their pay is reviewed against the new location's band, and their role must be possible from the new location.

## Moves to Toronto

Hold all approvals for relocations **into Toronto** until further notice. Contact the Head of People before discussing any Toronto move with an employee or candidate.
