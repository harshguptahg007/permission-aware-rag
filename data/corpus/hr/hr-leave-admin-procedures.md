# Leave Administration Procedures

**Owner:** HR operations
**Audience:** People team. Employees should read the Leave Policy (POL-HR-001).

This document explains how the People team processes leave in PeopleHub and payroll. It implements POL-HR-001; if the two disagree, the policy wins.

## 1. PTO

- PeopleHub accrues **1.67 days per month** automatically for full-time employees.
- Approvals are done by managers; HR operations only steps in for disputes or system errors.
- **Carry-over job:** runs on 1 January. It moves up to 5 unused days into a `carryover` bucket and forfeits the rest. A second job on **1 April** forfeits unused carry-over days.
- UK and Ontario employees: check the forfeiture report against statutory minimums before the job runs. Where statutory leave would be lost, pay it out instead (payroll code `PTO-STAT`).

## 2. Sick leave

- 10 days per year, reset on 1 January.
- When an employee reaches **5 consecutive sick days**, PeopleHub creates a task for the People partner to:
  1. request a doctor's note;
  2. check short-term disability eligibility (100% of salary for up to 12 weeks);
  3. contact the employee to offer support.
- Do not ask employees for medical details beyond what the insurer needs. Store medical documents in the restricted *Medical* folder of the employee file only.

## 3. Parental leave

1. Employee submits a parental leave request at least 8 weeks ahead (when possible).
2. HR operations confirms dates and type (primary 16 weeks / secondary 8 weeks).
3. Payroll setup:
   - **US:** full salary continued by Acme (code `PAR-FULL`).
   - **Canada:** employee applies for EI maternity/parental benefits; Acme pays a top-up to 100% (code `PAR-TOPUP-CA`). Send the Record of Employment within 5 days.
   - **UK:** Statutory Maternity/Paternity/Shared Parental Pay plus Acme top-up to 100% (code `PAR-TOPUP-UK`).
4. Set the **phased return** flag for primary caregivers (60% hours on full pay for 4 weeks).
5. Benefits continue unchanged during parental leave.

## 4. Bereavement, jury duty, floating holidays

- Bereavement: up to 5 days (immediate family), 2 days (extended). No evidence required.
- Jury duty: employee uploads the summons; full pay continues.
- Floating holidays: 2 per year, reset on 1 January, no carry-over.

## 5. Unpaid leave

- Needs manager **and** Head of People approval in PeopleHub.
- Benefits continue for the first 30 days. After that, US employees are offered COBRA-equivalent continuation; Canada and UK follow the plan rules.
- Unpaid leave pauses PTO accrual and equity vesting (check the equity plan terms with Finance).

## 6. Leaving employees

- **US:** accrued unused PTO is paid out on termination in states that require it; otherwise per the offer letter.
- **Canada and UK:** accrued statutory holiday is always paid out.
- Carry-over and floating holidays are not paid out unless required by law.

## 7. Senior leadership

Leave arrangements for VP-level and executive employees are managed directly by the Head of People and are not processed through the standard approval flow. Do not change leave settings on executive records without a ticket from the Head of People.

## 8. Audits

HR operations runs a quarterly audit comparing PeopleHub balances with payroll for a random sample of 25 employees.
