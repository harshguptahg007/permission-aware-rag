# Visa Sponsorship Process

**Owner:** People team
**Immigration counsel:** Harbor & Lane LLP

## When we sponsor

Acme sponsors work visas for roles that are hard to fill locally, mainly senior engineering, data and security roles. Sponsorship must be approved by the department head and the Head of People **before** an offer is made.

## Visa types we support

| Country | Visa | Typical timeline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| US | **H-1B** (new or transfer) | Transfer: 2–4 months; new: lottery in March | Premium processing paid by Acme |
| US | **TN** (Canadian and Mexican citizens) | 2–6 weeks | For eligible professions, e.g. engineers |
| US | **L-1** (intra-company transfer) | 2–4 months | After 1 year employed at a non-US Acme entity |
| Canada | Global Talent Stream work permit | 1–3 months | — |
| UK | **Skilled Worker** visa | 1–3 months | Acme holds a sponsor licence |

## Green cards

Acme starts the US green card (PERM) process for employees after **12 months** in a role, with a rating of 3 or above in their most recent review. Costs are paid by Acme as required by law.

## Costs

- Acme pays all legal and government fees for the employee.
- Dependants' visa fees are also covered.
- If an employee leaves within **12 months** of a new visa being approved, they repay the premium processing fee (US) or the Immigration Skills Charge is not recoverable (UK).

## Process

1. Hiring manager flags the need in the requisition.
2. Recruiter checks basic eligibility with Harbor & Lane (free initial assessment).
3. After offer acceptance, the People team opens a case with Harbor & Lane.
4. Employee uploads documents to the Harbor & Lane portal.
5. HR operations tracks expiry dates in PeopleHub and starts renewals **6 months** before expiry.

## Layoffs and visa holders

If a sponsored employee's role is affected by any restructuring, Legal and Harbor & Lane must be consulted before the employee is told, because of the 60-day grace period (H-1B) and sponsor licence reporting duties (UK).
