# Exit Interview and Offboarding Process

**Owner:** People team

## When someone resigns

1. The manager tells the People partner **the same day** and records the resignation in PeopleHub.
2. The People partner confirms the notice period:
   - US: 2 weeks requested (not legally required).
   - Canada: per the employment agreement, minimum statutory notice.
   - UK: per the contract, usually 1 month (3 months for directors).
3. For high performers or critical roles, the manager and People partner decide within 48 hours whether to make a counter-offer. Counter-offers need Head of People approval.

## Exit interview

- Offered to every leaving employee, run by a **People partner** (never the employee's manager).
- Held in the final 2 weeks, 30 minutes, by video call.
- Standard questions:
  - Why are you leaving? What would have made you stay?
  - How was your relationship with your manager?
  - Did you have the tools and information you needed?
  - Would you recommend Acme as a place to work?
- Answers are recorded in PeopleHub in the *Exit* section, which is visible only to the People team.
- Quarterly themes (not individual answers) are shared with department heads.

## Offboarding checklist

- [ ] Final pay calculated, including accrued PTO payout where required.
- [ ] Equity: send the departing employee the exercise-window summary (90 days for options after leaving).
- [ ] Benefits end date communicated; COBRA (US) information sent.
- [ ] IT ticket "Leaver" created for access removal at **end of the last working day**.
- [ ] Laptop return arranged (prepaid courier box for remote employees).
- [ ] Manager reassigns open work and on-call shifts.
- [ ] Remove from Slack channels and calendar groups.

## Involuntary exits

Terminations for performance or misconduct, and any reduction in force, follow separate procedures owned by the People team leadership and Legal. Exit interviews are optional in those cases.

## Re-hire

Employees who leave in good standing are eligible for re-hire. The People partner marks eligibility in PeopleHub.
