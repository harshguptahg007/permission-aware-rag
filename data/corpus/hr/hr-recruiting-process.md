# Recruiting Process

**Owner:** People team (Recruiting)
**Audience:** People team and recruiters. Hiring managers get the summary version in the manager toolkit.

## 1. Opening a role

1. The hiring manager submits a **requisition** in PeopleHub with the job family, level, location and justification.
2. Finance confirms the role is in the approved headcount plan.
3. The People partner checks the level against the job architecture.
4. The requisition is approved by the department head. **Backfills** follow the same path; in 2027, engineering backfills also need VP approval.

No role is advertised before the requisition is approved.

## 2. Sourcing

- All roles are posted on the careers page and at least one job board.
- Internal candidates can apply to any open role after **12 months** in their current role, and must tell their manager.
- **Employee referral bonus:** $3,000 (US), CAD 3,500, £2,250, paid after the new hire completes 90 days. Referrals for director roles and above don't qualify.
- Agencies are used only with Head of People approval. Fees above **15%** of first-year base need Finance approval.

## 3. Interview loop

| Stage | Who | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | Recruiter | 30 min |
| Hiring manager screen | Hiring manager | 45 min |
| Skills interviews | 2–3 interviewers from the structured rubric | 60 min each |
| Values interview | Trained interviewer from another department | 45 min |
| Debrief | Hiring manager, interviewers, recruiter | 30 min |

- Interviewers submit written feedback in PeopleHub **within 24 hours** and before the debrief.
- Every interviewer must complete the interviewer training, including the bias module.
- Decisions are made in the debrief by the hiring manager. No single interviewer can veto.

## 4. Offers

- Offers are built from the salary band for the role's family, level and location. Bands are visible to recruiters and the People team only.
- Standard offers sit between the band **minimum and midpoint**.
- Offers **above the midpoint** need approval from the Head of People.
- Offers **above the band maximum** are not allowed. If a candidate needs more, the role may be re-levelled.
- Sign-on bonuses up to $10,000 need director approval; above that, Head of People.
- Equity at hire follows the equity grant table for the level.

Recruiters never share band figures in writing with candidates. You may share the posted salary range where pay transparency laws require it (for example, for roles open to candidates in certain US states).

## 5. After acceptance

- Run a background check (after the offer is accepted, where local law allows).
- Hand over to HR operations to start the onboarding checklist.
- Close the requisition and notify all other candidates within 5 working days.

## 6. Metrics

Recruiting reports monthly on time-to-fill (target **45 days**), offer acceptance rate (target **85%**), and pipeline diversity at each stage.
