# 2027 Equity Refresh Programme

**Confidential. Head of People, CFO and CEO only. Board approval required.**

## Purpose

Most employees' initial option grants finish vesting after 4 years. Refresh grants keep long-tenured, high-impact people invested in Acme.

## Pool

- Total 2027 refresh pool: **1.8 million options**, about **1.2%** of fully diluted shares.
- Strike price: the 409A valuation at the grant date (latest valuation: **$6.40** per share, October 2026).
- Vesting: 4 years, no cliff, monthly.

## Eligibility

- At least **2 years** of service by 1 March 2027.
- Most recent review rating of **4 or 5**, or a rating of 3 with a critical-role flag.
- Not on a PIP or under an open investigation.

## Guideline grants (options)

| Level | Rating 3 + critical | Rating 4 | Rating 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software Engineer II / equivalent | 2,000 | 4,000 | 6,000 |
| Senior | 3,500 | 7,000 | 10,000 |
| Staff | 6,000 | 12,000 | 18,000 |
| Principal / Director | 10,000 | 20,000 | 30,000 |

## Interaction with restructuring

Employees whose roles are affected by the 2027 restructuring will **not** be nominated for refresh grants. Department heads must check nominations against the restructuring list before submitting.
