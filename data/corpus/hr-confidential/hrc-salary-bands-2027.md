# 2027 Salary Bands

**Strictly confidential. Head of People and authorised compensation staff only.**
**Effective:** 1 March 2027 (aligned with the merit cycle)
**Basis:** 65th percentile of market (POL-HR-004), benchmarked using the two annual software-industry salary surveys, October 2026 data.

Bands are base salary only. Minimum / midpoint / maximum.

## 5. Changes from 2026

- Engineering bands increased by **4%** on average; Staff and Principal by **6%** (market pressure).
- Finance and People bands increased by **3%**.
- Toronto bands increased by **2%** only. *(Note: Toronto bands may become irrelevant depending on the 2027 office decision. Do not publish to Toronto managers until confirmed.)*
- London bands unchanged.

## 6. Merit budget 2027

- Merit pool: **3.5%** of base payroll.
- Promotion pool: separate **1.0%** of base payroll.
- Indicative merit by rating: 5 → 7%, 4 → 5%, 3 → 3%, 2 → 1%, 1 → 0%. Employees above band midpoint receive 75% of the indicative figure.

## 7. Employees outside their band

- **Below minimum:** 11 employees company-wide. Brought to minimum on 1 March 2027 regardless of rating.
- **Above maximum:** 6 employees. Merit paid as a one-off lump sum instead of a base increase.
