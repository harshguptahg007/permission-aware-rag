# 2027 Reduction in Force: Selection Criteria and Process

**Strictly confidential. Head of People, CEO, CFO and General Counsel only.**
**Status:** Plan approved in principle by the board on 22 October 2026; these selection criteria approved by leadership on 4 November 2026. Final board approval expected December 2026.
**Related:** Office Consolidation Plan 2027 (exec folder).

## 1. Summary

As part of the 2027 operating plan, Acme will reduce headcount by about **8% (approximately 34 roles)** and **close the Toronto office** by the end of **Q2 2027**.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Roles affected | ~34 (of ~420) |
| Announcement date | **Wednesday 24 February 2027** |
| Toronto office closure | **30 June 2027** |
| Toronto employees | 58; about 22 roles eliminated, about 36 offered remote-Canada or relocation to Austin |
| Other locations | ~12 roles across Austin, London and remote-US |

## 2. Principles

1. Decisions are based on **roles**, not people, wherever possible.
2. Criteria are written down and applied consistently, then reviewed by Legal for adverse impact.
3. Nobody is selected because they raised a complaint, took protected leave, or are on a visa.
4. Affected employees hear the news from their manager and a People partner, in person or by video, on the announcement day.

## 3. Selection criteria

### Step 1: Role elimination
Department heads identify roles that are no longer needed under the 2027 plan (for example, duplicated functions between Toronto and Austin, reduced hiring means fewer recruiters, Ledger partner integrations moving in-house).

### Step 2: Pool selection
Where several people hold the same role and only some roles are eliminated, the **selection pool** is everyone in that role and location. Within a pool, employees are scored:

| Criterion | Weight | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Last two review ratings | 50% | PeopleHub |
| Critical skills for the 2027 roadmap | 30% | Department head assessment, written justification |
| Breadth (ability to cover other roles) | 20% | Manager assessment |

**Tenure is not a criterion**, except as a tie-breaker (longer tenure kept) where local law requires.

### Step 3: Legal review
Legal reviews the full list for adverse impact by age, gender, ethnicity and disability, and checks:
- employees on or recently returned from protected leave;
- employees with open investigations or complaints (for example INV-2026-06);
- visa holders (Harbor & Lane consulted);
- statutory consultation duties (UK collective consultation is **not** triggered, as fewer than 20 UK roles are affected).

## 4. Severance (non-executive)

| Location | Package |
|---|---|
| US | **4 weeks + 2 weeks per year of service** of base pay; 3 months of COBRA premiums paid; accelerated vesting of options that would vest in the next 3 months |
| Canada | Statutory notice and severance under Ontario law **plus** the US-equivalent formula, whichever is greater |
| UK | Statutory redundancy pay plus enhanced payment matching the US formula; payment in lieu of notice |

All affected employees also receive outplacement support for 3 months and keep their laptop.

## 5. Communication plan

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| Dec 2026 | Board approval |
| 1–20 Feb 2027 | Final lists, Legal review, packages prepared |
| 23 Feb 2027 | Managers of affected employees briefed (under confidentiality) |
| **24 Feb 2027** | Affected employees told 08:00–12:00 local; CEO all-hands at 14:00 CT |
| 24 Feb 2027 | Access changes for affected employees at end of day (garden leave with pay through notice period) |
| Mar–Jun 2027 | Toronto office wind-down |

## 6. Confidentiality

Until 24 February 2027, the plan is known only to the leadership team, the People team leadership and Legal. Do not refer to it in hiring decisions, relocations, refresh grant nominations or promotion approvals except through the Head of People.
