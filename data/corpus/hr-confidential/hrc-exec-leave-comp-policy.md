# Executive Leave & Compensation Policy

**Confidential. Head of People, CEO and the board's Compensation Committee only.**
**Applies to:** VP-level and above ("executives"). Supplements the company Leave Policy (POL-HR-001) and Compensation Philosophy (POL-HR-004).
**Approved by:** Compensation Committee, September 2026.

## 1. Leave

### 1.1 Paid time off
- Executives have **unlimited PTO** instead of the 20-day accrual in POL-HR-001.
- Expected minimum: 15 days per year. The Head of People reviews executives who take fewer than 10 days.
- Executive PTO is **not tracked** for payout purposes; nothing is paid out on leaving.

### 1.2 Sabbatical
- After **4 years** of continuous service as an executive or in a role that became executive, executives may take an **8-week paid sabbatical**.
- The sabbatical must be taken within 2 years of becoming eligible and needs CEO approval (Compensation Committee approval for the CEO).
- A sabbatical can be combined with PTO for up to 10 weeks in total.

### 1.3 Parental and sick leave
Same as POL-HR-001 (16/8 weeks parental, 10 sick days), with no executive-specific changes.

## 2. Compensation

### 2.1 Base salary and bonus targets (2027)

| Role | Base salary | Annual bonus target (% of base) |
|---|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer | $420,000 | 50% |
| Chief Financial Officer | $340,000 | 40% |
| General Counsel | $310,000 | 35% |
| VP Engineering | $300,000 | 35% |
| Head of People | $260,000 | 30% |
| VP Sales | $250,000 | 100% (commission-based OTE) |

- Executive bonuses are paid on company targets (revenue, net burn) set by the board, with a multiplier of 0–150%.
- Unlike other employees, executives are on an **individual** bonus plan.

### 2.2 Equity
- Executive equity grants and refreshes are approved by the board.
- **Double-trigger acceleration:** 100% of unvested equity vests if Acme is acquired *and* the executive is terminated without cause or resigns for good reason within 12 months.

## 3. Severance

| Situation | Severance |
|---|---|
| Termination without cause | **12 months** of base salary + pro-rated bonus at target + 12 months of health benefits |
| Change of control (double trigger) | 12 months of base + 100% of target bonus + full equity acceleration |
| Resignation or termination for cause | None |

Standard employee severance (4 weeks + 2 weeks per year of service) does not apply to executives.

## 4. Other executive benefits

- Annual executive health assessment (up to $2,500).
- Financial planning allowance: $5,000 per year.
- Travel: business class for flights over 4 hours (see the Executive Expense & Travel Exceptions document held by Finance).
- Home-office and phone fully covered.

## 5. Disclosure

These terms are **not** published in the company handbook. If employees ask whether executives have different leave or pay terms, the approved answer is: "Executive terms are set by the board's Compensation Committee."
