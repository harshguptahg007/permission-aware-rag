# Whistleblower Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-GEN-009
**Owner:** Legal

## What you can report

Any genuine concern about wrongdoing at Acme, for example:

- Fraud, bribery or misuse of company money (including expenses)
- Breaches of law or regulation, including data protection
- Health and safety risks
- Harassment or discrimination that hasn't been handled through normal channels
- Attempts to cover up any of the above

## How to report

You can choose any of these channels:

1. **Your manager** or skip-level manager.
2. **The General Counsel** (Grace Liu), directly or by email.
3. **The ethics hotline**, run by an independent provider. It's available 24/7 by phone or web, and you can stay **anonymous**.
4. **The chair of the board's Audit Committee**, for concerns about senior leadership or financial reporting.

## What happens next

- You receive an acknowledgement within **5 working days** (anonymous reports get a reference number to check status).
- Legal decides who investigates. Nobody involved in the concern will investigate it.
- Investigations normally finish within **60 days**. You'll be told when it's closed, though you may not be told the outcome if it involves another person's confidential information.

## Protection from retaliation

Acme will not tolerate retaliation (dismissal, demotion, exclusion, harassment) against anyone who raises a concern **in good faith**, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation is itself a breach of the Code of Conduct (POL-GEN-006).

Knowingly making false reports is a disciplinary matter.

## Confidentiality

Reports are handled confidentially and shared only with the people who need to know to investigate.

## External reporting

Nothing in this policy stops you from reporting concerns to a regulator or law enforcement.
