# Compensation Philosophy

**Policy ID:** POL-HR-004
**Owner:** People team

## What we believe

- Pay should be **fair, consistent and explainable**. Two people doing the same job at the same level, in the same location, should be paid in the same range.
- Pay should reward **impact**, not negotiation skill.
- We are open about **how** pay is set, even though individual salaries and band figures are kept confidential.

## How pay is set

### Market position
Acme targets the **65th percentile** of the market for base salary. We benchmark using two external salary surveys of software companies of similar size and stage, refreshed every year.

### Levels and bands
Every role is mapped to a **job family** (for example, Software Engineering or Finance) and a **level**. Each family-level-location combination has a salary band with a minimum, midpoint and maximum.

- New hires are usually offered between the band minimum and midpoint.
- Moving above the midpoint is based on sustained strong performance.
- Band figures are reviewed every year in Q4 and published to managers and the People team only.

### Location
Bands are set per location (Austin, Toronto, London, and remote-US). If you move to a different location, your pay is reviewed against the new location's band.

## Pay changes

| Change | When |
|---|---|
| Merit increase | 1 March each year, based on the H2 review rating and your position in band |
| Promotion increase | 1 March, alongside merit |
| Market adjustment | Ad hoc, if the annual benchmark shows a band has fallen behind |

## Equity

Most full-time employees receive stock options at hire. Additional equity may be granted at promotion or through periodic refresh grants. The details of equity refreshes are shared individually.

## Bonus

Sales roles have commission plans. Other roles do not have an individual bonus. A company-wide bonus may be paid in years when company targets are met; the board approves the amount.

## Pay equity

The People team runs a pay-equity review every year, comparing pay across gender and ethnicity within each level. Unexplained gaps are corrected in the next merit cycle.

## Questions

Ask your manager first: they can see your band and explain where you sit in it. You can also ask your People partner.
