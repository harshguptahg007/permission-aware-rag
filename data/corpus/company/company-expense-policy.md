# Expense Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-FIN-002
**Owner:** Finance
**Applies to:** All employees spending money on Acme's behalf.
**Last reviewed:** September 2026

## 1. Principles

Spend Acme's money as if it were your own. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary for business, and supported by a receipt. When in doubt, ask your manager **before** you spend.

## 2. How to submit expenses

- All expenses are submitted in **Spendly**, either from the mobile app or the web.
- Submit within **30 days** of the expense date. Claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed.
- Attach an itemised receipt for any expense **over $25**. For expenses under $25, a short description is enough.
- Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## 3. Approval limits

| Total claim | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,000 | Your direct manager |
| Over $2,000 | A VP in your reporting line |
| Any software subscription | Manager + IT (see section 7) |

Approvers check that the spend is business-related, within limits, and coded to the right cost centre.

## 4. Meals

| Location | Daily limit (per person) |
|---|---|
| Domestic travel | $75 |
| International travel | $100 |

- Limits include tax and tip.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only at team events approved by a director, and never as a stand-alone expense.
- Client meals: up to $120 per person. List all attendees and their companies in Spendly.

## 5. Accommodation

- Standard hotel cap: **$250 per night**.
- High-cost cities (London, New York, San Francisco): **$325 per night**.
- Book through **TripDesk** where possible (see the Travel Booking Guide, POL-FIN-008).
- Upgrades, minibar, in-room movies and laundry for stays under 5 nights are not reimbursable.

## 6. Air travel

- Flights **under 6 hours**: economy class.
- Flights of **6 hours or more**: premium economy.
- Book at least 14 days ahead where possible.
- Seat selection fees and one checked bag are reimbursable.
- Loyalty points stay with you, but should not influence which flight you book.

## 7. Software and subscriptions

Do not buy software with a personal card. Request it through the IT portal so IT can check security and licensing. Any SaaS tool that will hold customer data needs a security review before purchase.

## 8. Not reimbursable

- Personal travel extensions (extra hotel nights, side trips)
- Traffic or parking fines
- Gym memberships (covered separately by the wellbeing benefit)
- Gifts to government officials (see the Code of Conduct, POL-GEN-006)
- First-class or business-class travel upgrades

## 9. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times a year can request an Acme corporate card from Finance. Corporate card transactions must still be reconciled in Spendly within 30 days. Personal use of a corporate card is not allowed and may lead to the card being withdrawn.

## 10. Misuse

Deliberate misuse of expenses is a disciplinary matter and may lead to dismissal. If you see something that looks wrong, report it to Finance or through the whistleblower process (POL-GEN-009).
