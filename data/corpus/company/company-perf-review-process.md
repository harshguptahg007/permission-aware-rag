# Performance Review Process

**Policy ID:** POL-HR-003
**Owner:** People team

## Overview

Acme runs performance reviews **twice a year**:

| Cycle | Self-review opens | Cycle closes |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | 1 June | 30 June |
| H2 | 15 November | 15 December |

Reviews happen in **PeopleHub**. Everyone who has been at Acme for at least 3 months at the start of a cycle takes part.

## Steps

1. **Self-review (1 week).** You write what you delivered, how you worked with others, and what you want to grow in.
2. **Peer feedback (1 week).** You pick 2–4 peers; your manager may add one more. Peer feedback is shared with you without names.
3. **Manager review (1 week).** Your manager writes an assessment and proposes a rating.
4. **Calibration (1 week).** Managers in each department meet to compare proposed ratings so the same standard applies across teams. Ratings can move up or down in calibration.
5. **Conversation.** Your manager shares your final rating and written review in a 1:1.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 – Exceptional | Far above expectations; rare |
| 4 – Exceeds expectations | Consistently above the level |
| 3 – Meets expectations | Solid, reliable performance at the level |
| 2 – Partially meets | Some gaps; a development plan is agreed |
| 1 – Below expectations | Significant gaps; a formal improvement plan may follow |

Acme doesn't use forced distribution, but calibration expects most people to land at 3.

## Promotions

- Promotions are decided **only in the H2 cycle**.
- Your manager writes a promotion case showing you have been working at the next level for at least two quarters.
- Promotion cases are reviewed in calibration and approved by the department head and the People team.
- Promotions take effect on **1 March**, together with merit increases (see POL-HR-004).

## Links to pay

Ratings feed into the annual merit increase. The exact percentage for each rating isn't published, because it depends on the yearly budget and where you sit in your pay band.

## Appeals

If you disagree with your rating, raise it first with your manager, then with your People partner within **14 days** of your review conversation. The People partner will review the case with your skip-level manager.
