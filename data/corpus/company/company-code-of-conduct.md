# Code of Conduct

**Policy ID:** POL-GEN-006
**Owner:** Legal (Grace Liu, General Counsel)

This code applies to every Acme employee, contractor and board member, in the office, online, at customer sites and at company events.

## 1. Respect

- Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination based on race, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, national origin or any other protected characteristic are not tolerated.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.

## 2. Conflicts of interest

You have a conflict of interest when a personal interest could influence a decision you make for Acme. Examples:

- Owning more than 1% of a supplier, customer or competitor.
- Hiring or managing a close relative or partner.
- Taking a second job with a competitor.

Disclose any possible conflict to your manager and Legal using the conflicts form on the Legal intranet page. Disclosure is not a confession; most conflicts can be managed.

## 3. Gifts and hospitality

- You may accept gifts or hospitality worth **up to $100** per person per year from a business contact.
- Anything above that must be declared to Legal before you accept it.
- Never give or accept cash or cash equivalents (gift cards).
- **Never offer anything of value to a government official** without written approval from Legal.

## 4. Confidential information

- Customer data, financial results, unreleased product plans and personnel information are confidential.
- Only access information you need for your job, even if your access rights would let you see more.
- Don't discuss confidential matters in public places or on social media.

## 5. Insider trading

Acme is private, but you may learn confidential information about public companies (customers, partners). Do not trade on it or share it.

## 6. Use of company assets

Company equipment, accounts and money are for business use. Limited personal use of your laptop is fine (see POL-IT-007).

## 7. Speaking up

If you see behaviour that breaks this code, talk to your manager, the People team or Legal. You can also report anonymously through the whistleblower process (POL-GEN-009). Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a serious breach of this code.

## 8. Consequences

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and in some cases may be reported to the authorities.

Everyone must re-read and acknowledge this code every year in PeopleHub.
