# Welcome to Acme

We're glad you're here. This guide covers your first two weeks. Your manager and onboarding buddy will fill in the details for your team.

## Day 1

- Collect your laptop (Austin, Toronto and London offices) or receive it by courier (remote).
- Log in with the temporary password from IT, then set up **SSO and MFA** immediately (POL-IT-007).
- Join Slack. Start with **#general**, **#all-hands**, **#ask-people**, **#ask-it** and your team channel.
- Complete your profile and bank details in **PeopleHub**.
- Meet your **onboarding buddy**, a teammate who will answer the questions you don't want to ask your manager.

## Week 1

- Read and acknowledge the **Code of Conduct** (POL-GEN-006) and **IT Acceptable Use Policy** (POL-IT-007) in PeopleHub.
- Complete mandatory training: security awareness, privacy basics and anti-harassment.
- Enrol in benefits within **30 days** of your start date (POL-HR-011).
- Have a first 1:1 with your manager to agree 30/60/90-day goals.

## Week 2

- Attend the monthly **New Hire Orientation** with sessions from leadership on strategy, products and culture.
- Shadow customer calls or support tickets for at least one hour. Everyone at Acme does this, including engineers.
- Set up your home office and claim the $500 stipend if you're hybrid or remote (POL-HR-005).

## Probation

There is a **90-day probation period** in Canada and the UK, and a 90-day introductory period in the US. Your manager will check in at 30, 60 and 90 days.

## Who to ask

- Your onboarding buddy: anything.
- #ask-people: pay, benefits, leave.
- #ask-it: laptop, accounts, access.
- Your manager: priorities and goals.
