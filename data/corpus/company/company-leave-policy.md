# Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-HR-001
**Owner:** People team
**Applies to:** All Acme employees (US, Canada, UK). Contractors are covered by their agency agreements, not this policy.
**Last reviewed:** October 2026

## 1. Purpose

Acme wants people to rest, recover and take care of their families without worrying about their jobs. This policy explains the types of leave available, how much you get, and how to request it.

Where local law gives you more than this policy, local law wins. Country-specific notes are in section 9.

## 2. Paid time off (PTO)

- Full-time employees receive **20 days of PTO per calendar year**.
- PTO accrues monthly at **1.67 days per month**, starting on your first day.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata to their contracted hours.
- PTO can be used for vacation, personal errands, religious observance or anything else. You don't need to give a reason.

### 2.1 Carry-over

- You may carry over **up to 5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**. Unused carried-over days after that date are forfeited (except where local law requires payout).
- Days above the 5-day limit are forfeited on 1 January.

### 2.2 Requesting PTO

1. Submit the request in **PeopleHub** under *Time Off → Request*.
2. For 1–2 days, give at least **3 working days' notice**.
3. For 3 or more consecutive days, give at least **2 weeks' notice**.
4. Your manager approves or declines within 2 working days. If they don't respond, escalate to your skip-level manager.

Managers should only decline PTO for real business conflicts (for example, a launch or an on-call rotation gap) and should suggest alternative dates.

## 3. Sick leave

- Employees receive **10 paid sick days per year**, separate from PTO.
- Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.
- Tell your manager as early as possible on the day. Log the day in PeopleHub within one week.
- For more than **5 consecutive sick days**, a doctor's note is required and the People team will contact you about short-term disability options.

Sick leave can also be used to care for a sick child, partner or dependent family member.

## 4. Parental leave

| Type | Duration | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks | 100% of base salary |
| Secondary caregiver | 8 weeks | 100% of base salary |

- Applies to birth, adoption and foster placement.
- Leave must start within 12 months of the birth or placement.
- Leave can be taken in up to **two blocks**.
- Tell the People team at least **8 weeks** before your planned start date where possible.
- Employees returning from primary-caregiver leave can work a **phased return** at 60% hours on full pay for their first 4 weeks back.

## 5. Bereavement leave

- Up to **5 paid days** for the death of an immediate family member (partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or in-law).
- Up to **2 paid days** for extended family members or close friends.
- No documentation is required.

## 6. Floating holidays

Every employee gets **2 floating holidays per year** in addition to the public holiday calendar. Use them for cultural or religious days that matter to you. They do not carry over.

## 7. Jury duty and civic leave

Acme pays your full base salary during jury duty or required court appearances as a witness. Employees in the US also receive up to **4 hours of paid time** to vote on election days.

## 8. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to **3 months** may be granted for personal reasons. It requires approval from your manager and the Head of People. Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave of up to 30 days; after that, the People team will explain your options.

## 9. Country notes

- **Canada:** Statutory vacation and leave entitlements under provincial employment standards (Ontario) apply where they are more generous.
- **UK:** The 20-day PTO allowance is in addition to UK bank holidays. Statutory maternity, paternity and shared parental leave rules apply; Acme tops up statutory pay to 100% of salary for the durations above.

## 10. Questions

Ask in **#ask-people** on Slack or email the People team. Questions about how leave is processed in payroll are handled by HR operations.
