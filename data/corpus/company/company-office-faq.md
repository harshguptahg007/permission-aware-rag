# Office FAQ: Wi-Fi, Printers and Facilities

Answers to the questions the workplace team gets most often. Edits welcome: this page is open for comments from all staff.

## Wi-Fi

**Which network should I use?**
Use **Acme-Corp** on your Acme laptop. It authenticates with your SSO certificate automatically, so there's no password.

**What about my phone or personal devices?**
Use **Acme-Guest**. The weekly password is posted on the screen at reception and in #office-austin, #office-toronto and #office-london.

**Wi-Fi is slow in a meeting room. What do I do?**
Post the room name in **#ask-it**. Large video calls work best on the 5 GHz band; most Acme laptops switch automatically.

## Printers

**How do I print?**
All printers use secure release. Send your job to **Acme-Print**, then tap your badge on any printer to release it. Jobs are deleted after 24 hours.

**Can I print in colour?**
Yes, but please keep it to customer-facing material.

**Who do I contact when a printer jams?**
The workplace team, via the facilities form on the IT portal (`it.acme.internal`).

## Badges and visitors

- Lost badges: report in #ask-it; a temporary badge is available at reception.
- Visitors must be registered in the visitor system at least **24 hours** in advance and must be escorted at all times.

## Kitchens and food

- Breakfast is provided on anchor days (Tuesday and Thursday).
- Lunch is catered on Thursdays in Austin, Toronto and London.
- Please label food in the shared fridges. Fridges are cleared every Friday at 16:00.

## Meeting rooms

Book rooms in the calendar. Rooms not checked into within 10 minutes are released automatically.

## Parking and bikes

- Austin: limited parking in the garage, first come first served. Bike storage on level P1.
- Toronto: no staff parking; transit subsidy available through PeopleHub.
- London: bike storage and showers in the basement.

## Lost and found

Items are kept at reception for 30 days, then donated.
