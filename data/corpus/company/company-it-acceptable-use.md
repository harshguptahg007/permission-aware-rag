# IT Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-IT-007
**Owner:** IT

## Scope

This policy covers Acme laptops, phones, accounts, networks and any SaaS tool you log into with your Acme identity.

## Accounts and passwords

- Use **single sign-on (SSO)** for every tool that supports it.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA)** is required on all accounts. Use the authenticator app or a hardware key; SMS codes are not allowed.
- Never share your password or MFA codes, including with IT. IT will never ask for them.
- Use the company password manager for any account that doesn't support SSO.

## Devices

- Laptops are managed by IT. Don't disable disk encryption, the endpoint agent, or automatic updates.
- Lock your screen when you step away. Screens auto-lock after 5 minutes.
- Report a lost or stolen device to **#ask-it** or the IT portal (`it.acme.internal`) **within 1 hour**.
- Personal phones may access email and Slack only if enrolled in the mobile management profile.

## Data handling

- Store work files in the company drive, not on your desktop or personal cloud accounts.
- Customer data must stay in approved production systems. Never copy it to a laptop, spreadsheet or personal tool.
- Don't paste confidential information, customer data or source code into **public AI tools**. Use the approved internal AI assistant, which keeps data within Acme's tenancy.

## Software

- Install software from the self-service catalogue. Anything else needs an IT request.
- Browser extensions that read page content need IT approval.

## Personal use

Limited personal use of your laptop and internet connection is fine, as long as it doesn't interfere with work, break the law, or involve offensive content.

## Monitoring

Acme monitors company devices and accounts for security purposes, in line with local law. Monitoring is for security, not for tracking productivity.

## Leaving Acme

On your last day, return your laptop and any other equipment. Access is removed at the end of your last working day.

## Breaches

Report suspected security incidents immediately in **#security**. Deliberate breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
