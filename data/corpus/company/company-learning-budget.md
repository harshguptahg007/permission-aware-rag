# Learning & Development Budget

**Policy ID:** POL-HR-010
**Owner:** People team

## How much

Every full-time employee gets **$1,500 per calendar year** for learning and development. Part-time employees get a pro-rata amount. New joiners receive a pro-rata amount for their first year.

The budget **does not roll over**. Unused budget is lost on 31 December.

## What you can use it for

- Online courses and learning platform subscriptions
- Books and e-books
- Conference tickets (travel to conferences is covered by your team's travel budget, not this one)
- Certifications and exam fees
- Workshops and coaching related to your role or a role you're growing into

## What it can't be used for

- Degrees or long-term academic programmes. These go through the separate education assistance process, which needs VP approval.
- Hardware or software not related to a specific course.
- Courses unrelated to any work skill.

## How to use it

1. Agree the learning with your manager (a Slack message is fine).
2. Pay with your personal card.
3. Claim in **Spendly** under the category *Learning & Development*, attaching the receipt.

For anything over **$500**, get your manager's approval *before* you pay.

## Learning time

You can spend up to **4 hours a month** of work time on learning. Block it in your calendar so your team knows.

## Sharing back

If you attend a conference, share a short write-up or a lightning talk with your team. It's not required, but it's encouraged.
