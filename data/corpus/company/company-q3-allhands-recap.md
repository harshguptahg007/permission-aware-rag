# Q3 2026 All-Hands Recap

*Held 8 October 2026, Austin office and live-streamed. Recording in the #all-hands channel.*

## Headlines from Henry (CEO)

- **Revenue grew strong double digits year over year** in Q3. Henry thanked the sales and customer success teams for a record quarter for renewals.
- **Acme Beacon passed 1,000 paying customers** in September.
- **Acme Ledger reached general availability** in August, and the first 40 customers are live in production.
- We are **slowing hiring in Q4** to focus on efficiency and on the roles that matter most for next year. Open roles that are already in final stages will continue.

## Product update (Priya, VP Engineering)

- Beacon: new log-retention tiers and the redesigned alerting UI shipped in Q3.
- Ledger: multi-currency invoicing is next, targeted for Q1 2027.
- Reliability: Priya talked openly about the September security incident (INC-2041). A public summary is on the company drive. The main fixes, such as default-private storage and automated bucket policy checks, are already in place.

## Go-to-market (Marcus, VP Sales)

- Three new enterprise logos in financial services.
- The partner programme launched with two consulting partners.

## People (Dave, Head of People)

- The H2 review cycle opens on **15 November**.
- Benefits open enrolment runs 1–15 November.
- New learning platform licences are available. Your $1,500 learning budget can be used for them.

## Q&A highlights

**Q: Are there going to be layoffs?**
A: Henry said there are no plans to share today, and that the Q4 focus is on hiring discipline and efficiency. He committed to being direct with people if anything changes.

**Q: Will the Toronto and London offices stay open?**
A: Henry said our office footprint is reviewed every year as part of planning, and any changes would be communicated well in advance.

**Q: Can you share the exact revenue number?**
A: Not at an all-hands. Detailed financials go to the board. Henry repeated that growth was "strong double digits".

**Q: When will Ledger support multi-currency?**
A: Q1 2027 is the target.
