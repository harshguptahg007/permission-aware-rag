# Acme Product Overview

A plain-language overview of what Acme sells, for every employee.

## Acme Beacon

**What it is:** an observability and log-monitoring platform delivered as SaaS.

**Who buys it:** platform and SRE teams at mid-size and enterprise software companies, increasingly in financial services and healthcare.

**What it does:**
- Collects logs, metrics and traces from customer applications through lightweight agents.
- Lets teams search logs in near real time and build dashboards.
- Sends alerts to Slack, PagerDuty or email when thresholds or anomaly rules trigger.
- Offers **log exports**: customers can export log data to files for audits or long-term storage.

**Plans:** Starter, Team and Enterprise. Enterprise adds single sign-on, longer retention, audit logs and a dedicated support engineer.

**Milestone:** passed 1,000 paying customers in September 2026.

## Acme Ledger

**What it is:** a billing and invoicing API for software companies.

**Who buys it:** product and finance teams that need usage-based billing without building it themselves.

**What it does:**
- Meters usage events and converts them into invoice line items.
- Generates and sends invoices, and handles payment retries.
- Provides revenue reports that finance teams can reconcile with their accounting system.

**Status:** generally available since **August 2026**. Multi-currency invoicing is planned for Q1 2027.

## How the two products relate

Beacon and Ledger are sold separately. Some customers use Ledger to bill for usage, and Beacon to monitor the systems that produce that usage. There is no bundle pricing today.
