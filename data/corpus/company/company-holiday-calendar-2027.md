## Company-wide quiet week

Acme observes a **quiet week from 27 to 31 December 2027**. Offices stay open, but no meetings are scheduled and on-call rotations are staffed by volunteers who receive time off in lieu.

## On-call and holidays

Engineers on call during a public holiday receive **one day of time off in lieu** per holiday worked, in addition to the standard on-call allowance.
