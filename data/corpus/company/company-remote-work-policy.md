# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-HR-005
**Owner:** People team

## Our model

Acme is a **hybrid** company. We have offices in **Austin** (headquarters), **Toronto** and **London**, and we also hire fully remote employees in the US.

## Anchor days

- Employees who live **within 50 miles of an Acme office** work from the office on **Tuesdays and Thursdays** ("anchor days").
- The other three days are flexible: office or home, your choice.
- Teams can agree extra in-office days for planning weeks or launches.

## Fully remote work

- New roles are advertised as hybrid or remote at the time of hiring.
- Existing hybrid employees who want to become fully remote need approval from their **VP**.
- Remote employees are expected to visit an office or attend a team off-site at least **twice a year**. Acme pays for the travel under the Expense Policy (POL-FIN-002).

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–15:00 in your team's primary time zone**. Outside core hours, work when it suits you, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Home office

- A one-time **$500 home-office stipend** for equipment like a chair, desk or monitor, claimed through Spendly.
- IT provides your laptop, a monitor and a headset.
- An internet allowance of $40 per month for fully remote employees.

## Working from another country

You can work from a country other than your employment country for up to **20 working days per year**, with manager approval. Longer stays need a review by the People and Legal teams because of tax and immigration rules.

## Moving

If you plan to move home, tell your manager and the People team **before** you move. A move can change your pay band location (see POL-HR-004) and your office anchor-day requirement.

## Office changes

Acme reviews its office footprint every year as part of planning. Any changes to office locations would be communicated with plenty of notice.
