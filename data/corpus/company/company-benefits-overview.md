# Benefits Overview

**Policy ID:** POL-HR-011
**Owner:** People team

This is a summary. The full plan documents in PeopleHub are the legal reference.

## Health

| Country | Provider | Employer contribution |
|---|---|---|
| US | Northwind Health (PPO and HDHP options) | 90% of employee premium, 75% for dependants |
| Canada | Northwind Health Canada (extended health + dental) | 100% of premium |
| UK | Private medical insurance via Northwind Health UK | 100% for employee, dependants optional at your cost |

Dental and vision are included in all plans. US employees on the HDHP plan receive an annual **$1,000 HSA contribution** from Acme.

## Retirement

- **US:** 401(k) with a **4% match**, vested immediately.
- **Canada:** Group RRSP with a **4% match**.
- **UK:** Workplace pension with a **5% employer contribution**.

## Life and disability

- Life insurance at 2× base salary.
- Short-term disability: 100% of salary for up to 12 weeks.
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary after 12 weeks.

## Wellbeing

- **$75 per month** wellbeing allowance, usable for gyms, fitness classes, apps or therapy.
- Free access to the employee assistance programme (confidential counselling, 6 sessions per issue).

## Learning

**$1,500 per year** learning and development budget. See POL-HR-010.

## Time off

See the Leave Policy (POL-HR-001): 20 days PTO, 10 sick days, 16/8 weeks parental leave, 2 floating holidays.

## Open enrolment

Open enrolment runs **1–15 November** each year. Outside that window, you can only change plans after a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, adoption, loss of other coverage). Report life events in PeopleHub within 30 days.

## Questions

For benefits questions, ask in #ask-people. For questions about a specific claim, contact Northwind Health directly.
